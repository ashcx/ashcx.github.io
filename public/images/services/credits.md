# Service illustrations

The photography and event-video service cards use stock photographs from Pexels. The social-media card uses an image supplied by the site owner.

- `photography.webp` and `photography-mobile.webp`: Nguyễn Tiến Thịnh — https://www.pexels.com/photo/photographer-captures-wedding-moment-on-camera-33072063/
- `event-video.webp` and `event-video-mobile.webp`: Hasan Gulec — https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-person-holding-a-video-camera-11480028/
- `social-media.webp` and `social-media-mobile.webp`: image supplied by the site owner on 2026-10-22, showing a smartphone recording a speaker at an event. Replaces the previous iStock agency preview.

Photography and event-video license reference: https://www.pexels.com/license/

Downloaded 2026-10-22. Cropped and resized to 16:10 desktop images and separate square mobile thumbnails, then converted to WebP. The event-video photo shows hands operating a professional video camera, with the operator's face outside the frame. The social-media image shows a portrait phone recording a speaker on stage. Separate crops centre the phone on desktop and mobile.

Photography and event-video exports were regenerated from original-size Pexels JPEGs at WebP quality 32 for desktop and 28 for mobile (effort 6, smart chroma subsampling). Display assets remain 960 × 600 and 216 × 216. Local originals and previous exports are retained under ignored output/ for future re-export.

The supplied social-media image was exported at the same dimensions and WebP settings: quality 32 for desktop, quality 28 for mobile, effort 6, and smart chroma subsampling.
